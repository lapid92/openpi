Status: preparation; no scored outcomes collected. The generated protocol.json and its committed SHA256 will freeze exact identities and lists before smoke and scored evaluation.

## Leader scope and acceptance criteria
Compare unchanged, frozen pi05 at fixed 1, 2, 4, 10 Euler steps on paired task instances, initial states, and chunk-indexed Gaussian noise. No training, predictor updates, or adaptive policy. Reuse pushed evaluation code at 2e2c93df5c75918b59cffc30f550e2ff717a2cb0 and its ancestors. All execution and durable outputs reside on Volt uz2ptakxucbe. Four physical H100 UUIDs are recorded in gpu-identity.csv and must be checked before model initialization.

LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus share the official LIBERO-tuned pi05 checkpoint, with full file-content hashes plus normalization/preprocessing identity. RoboCasa requires an independently verified completed RoboCasa-tuned pi05 checkpoint and its own observation/action transforms. Report each benchmark separately; no pooled policy success rate. Residual head logging is omitted to keep the fixed-production path primary.

## Outcome-independent design
No screening or selection from new outcomes. LIBERO: all 40 tasks across spatial, object, goal and long-horizon suites, ten declared initial states per task with seeds 1001 through 1010: 400 paired cases, 1600 scored episodes maximum. LIBERO-Plus: deterministic selection by registry metadata, covering four suites, severity 2 and 3, robot initial states, camera viewpoints and Objects Layout (the benchmark's object-position category) when available; distinct base task families, maximum 48 conditions with ten declared seed/state cases each (Objects Layout variants supply only one initial state, paired across ten distinct seeds; robot/camera conditions use indices 0–9): at most 480 pairs, 1920 scored episodes. The generated manifest enumerates every actual task and state; unavailable categories are documented before freezing, never replaced based on outcomes.
Smoke: disjoint seed 900001, one predetermined condition per available benchmark, all four arms. Smoke outcomes are reported separately and never replace main cases.

Use benchmark-specific standard action horizons; five actions executed per generated chunk, ten zero-motion stabilization actions, 256 render and 224 padded policy image size, standard 180-degree image rotation and LIBERO seven-action transform. Specify exact horizon per task in manifest. Pair each case on one GPU, rotate arm order deterministically across cases. Noise is a stable SHA256-keyed float32 Gaussian stream keyed by benchmark/task/seed/initial-state/chunk and independent of arm. Verify every noise hash, supplied and stabilized state hashes, first observation, checkpoint/preprocessing, and record completeness.

Success is task completion confirmed from simulator source, not a time-limit termination. Infrastructure failures are errors, preserved and halted for diagnosis, never counted as task failures or used to choose replacements. Stop at the fixed enumerated maximum regardless of intermediate success/failure/rescue counts. No significance-based or outcome-driven stopping. A corrected infrastructure attempt keeps the original error and exact case key.

## Analysis frozen before new results
Report per benchmark and task family: success at every arm, paired rescues (1 fails, extra arm succeeds), regressions (1 succeeds, extra fails), net success difference, and the fraction of one-step failures rescued. 95% percentile cluster bootstrap resamples whole tasks/conditions within the reporting population, 10000 draws, fixed seed; preserve all states and all arms within a cluster. Report cluster counts and warn when few clusters make uncertainty coarse; a one-cluster family has no meaningful between-cluster interval. Pairwise results remain descriptive, without post-hoc arm selection or multiplicity-unadjusted superiority claims.

If fewer than 20 one-step failures or fewer than 5 rescues are observed for an extra-step comparison, explicitly label rescue benefit unresolved for that benchmark. Always show exact counts, including regressions; absence of rescue does not establish equivalence.

Record per-chunk velocity evaluations, synchronized scored-policy latency (input transforms through output transforms), request latency, and simulator-inclusive episode latency with explicit boundaries. Warm all four compiled arms on smoke observations before scored timing. Report mean/median/p95 and total velocity evaluations; exclude parity/reference passes from scored cost and record them separately. No adaptive speedup claim.
